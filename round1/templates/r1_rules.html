TACTICAL RULES & SCORING MATRIX

Review the competition constraints, scoring algorithms, and anti-tamper protocols before initiating your assigned cyber challenges.

01

Challenge Allocation

  • Each registered team is assigned a deterministic set of 6 challenge variants drawn from the cyber fundamentals corpus.
  • Assignments are persisted server-side: refreshing the browser or re-authenticating seamlessly restores your active challenge state.
  • Categories span Ciphers, Packet Forensics, Steganography, Binary Obfuscation, and Protocol Decoding.
02

Timing & Point Valuation

  • Standard engagement duration is 30 minutes, synchronized authoritative to the server clock.
  • Each verified flag awards up to 100 points (maximum 600 total base score).
  • Points are allocated atomically upon cryptographic validation of the submission payload. Resubmitting an already solved challenge yields no duplicate points.
03

Flag Syntax & Hints

  • Submit findings matching standard format specifications (e.g., FLAG{...} or exact tokens).
  • Submissions are normalized for whitespace and evaluated case-insensitively.
  • Tactical intelligence hints are available per station; decrypting a hint incurs a fixed point mitigation deduction.
04

Anti-Tamper & Integrity

  • All submissions are audited against brute-force rate-limiting (burst limit triggers HTTP 429 cooling windows).
  • Timer calculations and challenge verifications execute strictly on the isolated server daemon.
  • Cooperation between opposing teams is prohibited. Maintain forensic custody and tactical discipline.
